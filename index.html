Shortkaar

Shortkaar is a private video tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It makes short videos and documentaries and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to YouTube channels the owner connects, at the posting times the owner sets.
  • Adds thumbnails, caption tracks, playlists and description links to those videos.
  • Reads the views and watch time of those videos, so it can learn which videos work best.
  • Reads comments on those videos and posts replies and a first comment on the owner's behalf.

Who can use it

Shortkaar is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, on channels the owner manages. Nobody else can sign in to it.
